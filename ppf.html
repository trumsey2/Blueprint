Key Features of Our PPF

Protection and Finish Options

We exclusively use premium-grade Verleno films to provide your vehicle with the ultimate shield against damage. Our Verleno paint protection film (PPF) is a state-of-the-art, self-healing, and optically clear layer that preserves your factory finish.

  • Self-Healing Technology: The advanced top coat automatically repairs light scratches and swirls when exposed to heat, keeping your finish looking flawless.
  • Exceptional Clarity: Our ultra-clear film ensures that your paint's true color and depth shine through without any distortion, haze, or yellowing over time.
  • Hydrophobic Properties: The film's surface repels water, dirt, and grime, making your vehicle easier to clean and maintaining its pristine look for longer.
  • Stain Resistance: Provides robust protection against environmental contaminants like bug splatter, bird droppings, and chemical etching.
  • Long-Term Warranty: Our installations are backed by a comprehensive manufacturer's warranty for up to 10 years, guaranteeing against yellowing, cracking, or lifting.

Whether you want to maintain your factory shine or change the finish, we have a solution for you.
